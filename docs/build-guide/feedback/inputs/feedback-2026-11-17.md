## Decided by Ben (2026-11-18, after the sync)
Each step that builds one of these records it in `04-decisions-and-risks.md` §2a; the values stay `provisional: true` until Bailey confirms.
- **D1. "Pending" capital never blocks.** Pending is a normal capital state; it needs no justification and never stops a quote moving forward (G3 and every later gate). Was open decision 17. → FB-21.
- **D2. A lead may approve their own work.** An engineering lead who is also the assigned engineer may decide G2; a pricing lead who is also the assigned pricer may decide G3. Was open decision 3. The seeded segregation rules bar only the KAM (G1, G4) and the price approver (G4), so the build proves this with tests and fixes whatever stopped Jim on Nov 17. → FB-21 (G3), FB-22 (G2).
- **D3. Payback only on request.** No quote needs a payback model by default. The pricer or the pricing lead may request one; Finance is notified; when Finance uploads it, whoever requested it is notified, and the pricing lead(s) are notified as today. G3 waits for payback only while a request is open. → FB-21.
- **D4. Extra pricing cost inputs, all optional, placed by best guess** (from who raised and who knows each in the sessions): **stocking agreement** (Faron) → the KAM at intake, Commercial inputs (the customer asks for it); **special packaging that needs floor loading** (Faron) → Engineering's pack-out (floor loaded is a pallet type; a special-packaging note); **returnable dunnage cost** (Faron, Gemma) → Engineering's pack-out, shown when the KAM ticked returnable dunnage; **tariffs** (Jim, with mold and freight costs) → Engineering's tooling cost lines ("Tariffs / duties"). Was open decision 2 (less the markup). → FB-23, FB-24.
- **D5. No tooling markup unless one is noted.** Jim's offer letter form marks the customer-funded tooling total up by about 15 %; in QuoteFlow the customer tooling price is the customer-funded total × (1 + markup), and the markup is **0 % unless one is noted on the quote** (an optional per-item markup %, default 0 in config). Was N18-14. → FB-23.
- **D6. Container item numbers are free text** for now. Was open decision 12. → FB-23.
