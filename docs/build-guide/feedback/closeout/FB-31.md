**Built 2026-11-19. Notes and deviations:**
- **Migration** `0056_fb31_commercial_terms_governance.sql`, journal `idx` 56, `when` 1795150000003. Assigned 0057 / 1795150000004; renumbered at the rebase onto `main` after FB-28 merged (FB-28 carried no migration; the floor is 0055's 1795150000002, nothing reverted).
- **N19-07 routing.** `app_setting.terms_exception_routes`: an ordered list of tiers `{ codes, approverPermission, approverLabel, documentRequired }`. `approverPermission` comes from an allow-list (`terms.exception.decide`, `terms.exception.decide.senior`); an empty list, an empty tier, a code in two tiers or an unknown key is refused by the parser, and the Admin console also refuses a code that is not on `requested_payment_terms` (`adminConfig.ts`). A non-standard code no tier lists goes to the first tier. The approver's display label lives in the tier (there is no role-label catalogue).
- **New permission** `terms.exception.decide.senior`, Leadership only, a stand-in for the CFO Sarah Brennan named (no CFO role was added: Entra work and Ben's call). The `role_permission` and `gate_definition` CHECK constraints were regenerated (as 0050 did).
- **The decision route** now declares the `quote.read` floor, as the gate decisions do, because who decides depends on the record; the service enforces the tier (`planTermsExceptionDecision`). Registry, route inventory (`SERVICE_CHECKED_PERMISSIONS` lists both permissions) and the PostAward write snapshot updated. Allowed and denied for every persona role on both tiers: `terms-fb31.int.test.ts`; PostAward (no persona) in the domain test.
- **Recorded on the request.** `terms_exception_request.approver_permission` (text, CHECK in the allow-list) and `document_required` (boolean). **Deviation:** the prompt asked for one nullable text column; `document_required` was added too, so a config change cannot silently add or drop the document requirement of an open request. Both Fabric-safe (no jsonb, no arrays). Pending rows are routed by the migration from the then-current config: **open requests for Net 60 and above now wait for Leadership.** No notification is back-filled.
- **Requester bar** applies only where no role holding the tier's permission also holds `terms.edit` (`requesterBarApplies`): today the senior tier. The pricing lead may still decide a Net 45 request they entered (question 6 below). With the current personas only a multi-role user can hit the bar; the integration test simulates it.
- **Document.** New attachment kind `payment_terms_approval` (versioned, like `cer_proof`; pdf, docx, msg, eml, png, jpeg). Uploaded on the exception card by anyone with `attachment.upload` on the quote (the KAM, Sales, Pricing, Engineering); Leadership reads it but does not upload. Approving a request whose tier needs a document needs a current one uploaded at or after the request was opened, so a document from an earlier, rejected request does not count; rejecting needs none. It is not offered on the Attachments tab's dropdown (`attachment_kinds_by_place` unchanged).
- **Who is told.** The request's notice goes to the holders of the tier's permission only; the in-app and email text, the timeline entry, the intake chip, the Commercial tab badge and hint, the toast, the G4 warning and the Final Review blocker fallback say who decides, from the tier. No string is hard-coded per tier.
- **Cut:** the "Payment-terms exceptions waiting for you" list on the approvals page (no list of pending exceptions exists; it needs a new route and page section). Leadership finds a request through its notice. Question 5 below.
- **N19-08 funding.** Relabelled by guarded migration: Customer funded (lump sum), Customer funded (amortized) (`meta.requiresFinanceReview`), Pretium funded, To be determined (still the intake default, still unanswered at Final Review). `ZERO_CER` and `SHARED` set inactive (not deleted). Help text is the builder's draft. Selects on the Commercial tab and Capital terms now include the quote's own retired code, marked "(no longer offered)"; labels use `valueListLabel`; the intake select already did. The customer share still shows on an old Shared quote.
- **Amortized finance review.** A new `g4_additional_approvals` rule `amortized_finance_review`, seeded **active**: a Leadership sign-off ("Finance review (Leadership)") before G4 Approve. The migration adds it only where the setting still holds FB-08's seed. Final Review blockers now list missing extra sign-offs ("Final Review also needs: …"), which they did not before. The help line where funding is chosen says it needs a finance review. **The rule is on at once:** any quote already in Final Review whose funding is amortized cannot be approved at G4 until Leadership signs the finance review.
- **Not changed (as instructed):** `roi_required_when.fundingModels` and the offer letter's `customerFundedToolingModels` still exclude `AMORTIZED` (question 7). The offer letter prints the current label, so a regenerated letter's "Tooling funding:" line now reads the new label (Legal owns the letter).
- **N19-10 wording.** "Does the customer have inventory or safety stock requirements?" (Yes / No / Not known yet), the note placeholder, the intake review, quote facts, Final Review and the pipeline column ("Inventory / safety stock requirements", "Inventory / safety stock (weeks)").
