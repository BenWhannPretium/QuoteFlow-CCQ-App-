**Built 2026-11-19. Notes and deviations:**
- **Migration:** none. No table, column, permission, gate, outcome, notification type or setting changed. No route changed (the email deep links in `apps/api/src/services/emailContent.ts` are the only API change).
- **What was built.**
  - Tabs: **Overview · Engineering · Capacity · Quoting · Commercial terms · Attachments** (`apps/web/src/lib/quote-tabs.ts`). The Capacity tab stays mounted once opened, like Engineering and Quoting (UX-001).
  - **Engineering tab** opens with **Engineering recommendation** (read-only: plant, process and machine, platform, cavitation, cycle time, budgetary capital, the engineer's notes, per item; status "Not submitted yet" or "Submitted by … on …", or "… is an engineering lead: no submit needed"). It keeps the intake and product facts, the assignment, the engineer's cards and submit, the lead's status and actions, and `#decision`.
  - **Capacity tab** opens with **Capacity recommendation** (read-only: "Capacity is signed off by … on …" / "Capacity is blocked by … on …" / "Not signed off yet", the note, and each capacity row in one line: where, "n% loaded with this business" with the tab's warning words, and the practical capacity from FB-14's Capacity at a glance). Then a short header: who works capacity ("No scheduler assigned: the engineer covers capacity." when none), "Assigned on the Engineering tab" (a link; assignment stays there), and the engineer's solution the rows are calculated with. Then the lead review status, "Go to the decision on the Engineering tab" (for whoever may decide G2), the capacity evidence with Capacity at a glance, CSV and attach, the sign-off, and the scheduler's submit. On a multi-item quote the sign-off sits above the Capacity tab's own item switcher.
  - **One data source, one item.** Both tabs read the page's one quote query (`quoteQueryKey`), so a save on either shows on both with no extra fetch. The item on screen is shared through a small context on the quote page (`components/engineering/item-context.tsx`, `useEngineeringItem`): picking an item on one tab picks it on the other, and switching still asks first when that item has unsaved changes on either tab.
  - **Each reader opens on their own tab** (`defaultQuoteTab`, `engineeringTabFor`, `leadWaitingPart`): a reader who works capacity but not the solution (the production scheduler) opens Capacity; the engineer opens Engineering; a lead opens the part submitted to them (Capacity only when capacity alone is in; Engineering when the engineer's part is in, both are in, or nothing is). The next step's `part: 'capacity'` also opens Capacity.
  - **The work dot** (`tabsWithWork`): on Capacity when the next step's part is capacity or the reader may submit capacity now (`leadReview.canSubmitCapacity`: after a send-back to them, or a sign-off not yet submitted); on Engineering for the engineer's part and the lead's decision.
  - **Links:** `#capacity` opens Capacity; `#decision` and `?tab=engineering` behave as before, so every link already sent keeps working. `quote.capacity_assigned` and `quote.capacity_ready` (sent only to the assigned scheduler) open `?tab=capacity` in the bell and in email; `quote.capacity_blocked` keeps Engineering; `quote.part_submitted` opens Engineering in the bell (the in-app item carries no payload) and, in email, the tab of the part submitted (`payload.part`; the type is in-app only today, so no email is sent). The scheduler's **Capacity requests** rows link to `?tab=capacity`. The next-step button for the capacity part reads **Open the Capacity tab**.
  - **Two blocks:** Overview "At a glance" has an **Engineering** card (engineer, feasibility decided, the solution per item) and a **Capacity** card (scheduler, the sign-off with who, when and the note, each row in a line, the capacity notes), each linking to its tab. The Quoting tab has **Engineering summary** and a separate **Capacity** card (`quoting-capacity-group`: the sign-off, then the rows). Final Review has **Engineering solution** (with `CostEstimateSummary`) and **Capacity evidence** (`review-capacity`: the sign-off, then the rows) as two sections.
- **Deviations from the prompt.**
  - "Capacity at a glance headline": nothing called a headline existed, so the line per row is built from what is stored or calculated already: utilization after (the stored figure) and the practical capacity per year from `capacityAtAGlance` (FB-14). Nothing new is stored.

Before FB-30 the scheduler saw the solution card on the same tab; without it the "no cavitation" warning would point at nothing.   - `quote.part_submitted` (review, 2026-11-19): it goes to the *other* person, so the reviewer asked whether it should open the reader’s own tab instead. The step file says: "give it the tab of the part that was submitted if the notice carries that, otherwise the Engineering tab"; that is what is built (in-app: Engineering, the item carries no payload; email: the submitted part). Left for Bailey (question 5).

  - Only the scheduler's own list (**Capacity requests**) links to the Capacity tab; the full queue table and its "Open the Engineering tab" menu item are unchanged (the queue is the engineering lead's).
  - `packages/domain/src/next-step` and `apps/api/src/services/quote-next-step.ts` were not changed: the next step already carries `part: 'capacity'`. Noted, not changed: the domain stops giving the scheduler `part: 'capacity'` once capacity is signed off, even though they still have to submit it; the dot covers that case through `canSubmitCapacity`.
