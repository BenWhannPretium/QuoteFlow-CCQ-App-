**Commercial Capital Quoting web app · Embedded Intelligence build · MVP due Fri 2026-11-13**
Builder: Ben Whann · Requirements and product owner (interim): Bailey Schmitt · Workflow coordination: Tre Curry · Sponsor: Darren Miller · Asana: REQ-8815

## What we're building (one paragraph)
QuoteFlow replaces the legacy ERF application for new-business quotes that need engineering and/or capital. It gives every quote one governed record, linked to its Salesforce opportunity. A KAM completes a short guided intake (Sales-knowable fields only). A sales leader qualifies it, and the upfront engineering lead works it from a prioritized, aged queue. Engineering records the solution and capacity evidence. Pricing records the price approval, an approver releases the proposition, and the KAM records the customer outcome with a reason, then commits and hands off the win (Stage 6, added in B-26). Everything is role-scoped, enforced on the server, and audited, and everything still under business decision is configuration rather than code.
