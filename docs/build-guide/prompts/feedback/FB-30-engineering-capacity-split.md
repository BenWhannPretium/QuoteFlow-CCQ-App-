## What the code does today (read 2026-11-19 at `c9ea359`; verify before relying on it)
- **One tab.** `components/engineering/EngineeringTab.tsx` renders, in order (lines ~150-276): the quote-level header (`GoToDecision`, `IntakeFactsHeader`, `ProductFactsHeader`, `AssignmentCard` for both the engineer and the scheduler, `LeadReviewStatus`, `EngPartsStatus`, `SectionLockLine`); then, per item, the engineer's cards (`SolutionCard`, `EquipmentBomCard`, `PricingInputsCard`, two `CostLinesCard`s for tooling and new line, `LaborCard`, `PackOutCard`, `LegacyNotes`) with `PartSubmit` "Submit to engineering lead"; then the scheduler's cards (`CapacityCard` "Capacity evidence" with `CapacityGlanceTable` "Capacity at a glance (calculated from the row above)" in a `<details data-testid="capacity-glance">`, `CapacityEvidenceActions`, `CapacitySignoffCard`) with `PartSubmit` "Submit capacity to engineering lead"; then `LeadReviewActions` (send back, **Decide**). On a multi-item quote the `EquipmentBomCard` and `CapacitySignoffCard` sit above the `ItemSwitcher`; on a single item the BOM card is under `SolutionCard` and the sign-off after `CapacityCard`. Ownership is shown only as a label (`PartLabel` ~406-422).
- **No "recommendation" field exists.** The capacity verdict is `eng_review.capacity_signoff_status` (`approved` | `blocked`) with `_by`, `_at`, `_note` (required only to block), one value per quote kept on item 1's row. The engineer's free text is `tooling_notes` and the new `machine_platform` (the old notes are deprecated and shown under `LegacyNotes`). Per-row capacity text is `capacity_evidence.notes`. The feasibility call is the G2 `gate_decision` row (`outcome_key`, `note`). So "the engineering recommendation" today is the engineer's solution (plant, process and machine, cavitation, cycle time, budgetary capital, notes) and "the capacity recommendation" is the sign-off plus the evidence. **Do not invent fields to make them look like recommendations.**
- **Two parts already** (`packages/domain/src/lead-review/parts.ts`): `engineeringParts()` returns an engineer part and a nullable scheduler part (null when no scheduler is assigned: then the engineer's part covers capacity); each has its own submit; `ready` means every part is in; permissions already differ (`engReview.edit` EngLead and the assigned Engineer; `capacity.edit` EngLead, assigned Engineer, assigned ProductionScheduler; `capacity.signoff` EngLead and assigned ProductionScheduler). The routes are already separate (`apps/api/src/routes/v1/engineering.ts`: `/eng-review`, `/capacity-signoff`, `/capacity-evidence`, `/eng-review/submit-capacity`).
- **Where the two are shown together:** Overview "At a glance" `EngineeringGlance` (`components/quote/AtAGlance.tsx` ~190-280, test id `glance-engineering`, lists engineer, scheduler, "Feasibility decided", per item plant, process, cavitation, capital, "Engineer's notes (tooling)", "Capacity notes"); the Quoting tab's `FromEngineering` and `EngineeringSummary` (`components/pricing/QuotingTab.tsx` ~178 and ~435, the G2 decision and note plus a capacity list, test id `quoting-capacity`); Final Review's `EngineeringSection` ("Engineering solution and capacity evidence", `ReviewScreen.tsx` ~670, test id `review-capacity`, with `CostEstimateSummary`); the engineering queue (`routes/queue/engineering.tsx`: a scheduler sees "Capacity requests", row links go to `?tab=engineering`); notifications `quote.capacity_assigned`, `quote.capacity_ready` and `quote.capacity_blocked` open `?tab=engineering` (`notification-target.ts` ~13-17, `NotificationRow.tsx` ~73, `next-step.ts` ~82, `emailContent.ts` ~98).
- **Tabs and defaults:** Overview · Engineering · Quoting · Commercial terms · Attachments (`lib/quote-tabs.ts`: `QUOTE_TABS`, `ENGINEERING_WORK_PERMISSIONS` = `engReview.edit`, `capacity.edit`, `capacity.signoff`, `gate.G2.decide`; `defaultQuoteTab`; `tabForHash`; `tabsWithWork`). The tab bar is sticky with a dot on a tab where the reader has work (FB-25). Panels stay mounted across tabs so typed work is not lost (UX-001): keep that. `#decision` (FB-22) focuses the decision section on the Engineering tab.

## Build

### A. A Capacity tab
1. Tabs become **Overview · Engineering · Capacity · Quoting · Commercial terms · Attachments**. There is no per-viewer visibility rule today: `$quoteNumber.tsx` renders `QUOTE_TABS.map` for every viewer. The edits are `TAB_LABELS` (~77, typed `Record<QuoteTab, …>`), a `kept('capacity')` mount beside Engineering's and Quoting's, and `DECISION_TABS` (which stays as is); `?tab=capacity` is accepted automatically through `isQuoteTab`.
2. **Engineering tab** keeps: the product and intake facts, `AssignmentCard`, the engineer's cards, the engineer's submit, the lead's status line and actions, and the decision section (`#decision`, "Go to the decision"). **Capacity tab** gets: its own short header (who the scheduler is, or "No scheduler assigned: the engineer covers capacity"), the capacity evidence and "Capacity at a glance", the evidence actions (CSV and attach), the capacity sign-off (the capacity recommendation), the scheduler's submit, and a status line for the lead's review with a link to the decision on the Engineering tab. Move `CapacitySignoffCard` and the `PartSubmit` for capacity with it, **including the multi-item layout** (the sign-off is one per quote; on a multi-item quote it sits above the item switcher on the Capacity tab, and each tab has its own item switcher, kept in step. **There is no `?item=` query and no shared item state today:** the item index is `useState(0)` inside `EngineeringTab` (~120), so build the shared state (a small hook or context both tabs read), and keep UX-001's behaviour.)
3. **One data source.** Both tabs read through the same queries (a shared hook or context), so there is no second fetch and no save from one tab leaves the other stale. UX-001's behaviour holds: both stay mounted, unsaved typing on either tab warns before leaving (`unsaved-changes.ts` and its test).
4. **Assignment** stays on the Engineering tab (it assigns both people). The Capacity tab shows the scheduler read-only with a link.
5. **Two headers that say what each is** (words are code): the Engineering tab opens with "Engineering recommendation" (read-only: the solution's plant, process and machine, cavitation, cycle time, budgetary capital, platform and notes, with who submitted and when, or "Not submitted yet"); the Capacity tab opens with "Capacity recommendation" (read-only: the sign-off status, who and when, the note, and the capacity at a glance headline, or "Not signed off yet"). Rows with no value are left out.

### B. Open each reader on their own work
`defaultQuoteTab` has no lead-review or parts input today and `tabsWithWork` reads only `nextStep.place`, so add a `part` input to both (the next-step already carries `part: 'capacity'`, `packages/domain/src/next-step/index.ts` ~251, and the web `NextStepAction.tab` in `lib/next-step.ts` ~76-87 is typed `'engineering' | 'quoting'`: widen it). `defaultQuoteTab`: an assigned ProductionScheduler (holder of `capacity.signoff` who is not `engReview.edit`) opens **Capacity**; an engineer opens **Engineering**; a lead with both opens the tab whose part is waiting for them (a submitted part first; the engineer's part if both). `tabsWithWork`: a dot on Capacity when the reader's work is capacity (`capacity.edit` or `capacity.signoff`, or a capacity send-back), on Engineering when it is the engineer's or the lead's decision. `tabForHash`: a new `#capacity` opens the Capacity tab; `#decision` stays on Engineering; **old links keep working** (`?tab=engineering` still opens Engineering, including every link already sent in an email).
**Notification and next-step targets** (verified against `packages/domain/src/notifications/recipients.ts` ~445-460 and `events.ts` ~221; re-check): `quote.capacity_assigned` and `quote.capacity_ready` go **only to the assigned scheduler**, so both open the **Capacity** tab. `quote.capacity_blocked` goes to the assigned engineer, the engineering-lead role and the KAM (in-app only; its email says "rework the solution"), so it keeps opening **Engineering**. The lead-facing notices are `quote.submitted_to_lead` (keeps `?tab=engineering#decision`) and `quote.part_submitted` (to the other person when a part is submitted; it is in `ENGINEERING_TYPES` in `emailContent.ts` ~37-46 but has **no case in `notification-target.ts`**, so it falls to 'quote'): give it the tab of the part that was submitted if the notice carries that, otherwise the Engineering tab. Change the email links (`emailContent.ts` ~88 and ~98), `notification-target.ts`, `NotificationRow.tsx` (~73) and the next-step button the same way. The scheduler's queue rows ("Capacity requests") link to the Capacity tab. `emails/render.ts` and `quote-next-step.ts` hold no tab links (the reviewer found none): leave them unless a test shows otherwise.

### C. Two blocks where they are shown together
- **Overview "At a glance":** the Engineering card becomes two cards, **Engineering** (engineer, per item plant, process and machine, cavitation, cycle time, capital, platform, the engineer's notes) and **Capacity** (scheduler, the sign-off recommendation, who and when, the capacity notes and headline), each with a link to its tab. "Feasibility decided" (G2) stays with Engineering.
- **Quoting tab** (`FromEngineering`, `EngineeringSummary`): the engineering summary and the capacity list become two clearly headed groups.
- **Final Review** (`EngineeringSection`): "Engineering solution" and "Capacity evidence" become two sections with their own headings and test ids (keep `review-capacity` on the capacity section); `CostEstimateSummary` stays with Engineering.
- **One addition, read-only, from data that already exists:** the capacity block on Overview, Quoting and Final Review may show the capacity sign-off status, who and when, and the note, which none of the three shows today (they show evidence and notes only). Do not change what any of them show otherwise, only how it is grouped and headed. Rows with no value stay left out.
