### E. Capacity decided in one step; a block goes straight back (W13-17, W13-18)
1. **Block sends the work back.** "Block capacity" (scheduler or lead; note required) now, in one transaction: records the block, ends any submitted-to-lead state, returns the work to the **engineer** with the blocker's note (same mechanics and lock rules as `returnToAssignee`, reason "Capacity blocked"), writes a timeline entry "Capacity blocked: <note>" (add a timeline case for the sign-off activities) and notifies the engineer (in-app and email), the engineering lead when someone else blocked, and the KAM (in-app: "Capacity is blocked on <quote>; Engineering is reworking it"). New event `quote.capacity_blocked`. The quote stays in Engineering and active; no gate decision is recorded.
2. **Approve stays where it is for the scheduler.** The status stays `blocked` until capacity is approved again; G2 Advance stays refused while it is not approved.
3. **The lead decides capacity and feasibility in one step.** In "Decide feasibility", when capacity is not yet approved and the lead holds `capacity.signoff`, Advance, Advance with risk and (until FB-17 switches it off) Advance – capital required show a required checkbox "Capacity approved (I'm signing it off)". **Today the lead can't reach that dialog:** "Decide feasibility" appears only when `forwardReadiness(...).ready` (`apps/web/src/lib/decision-label.ts` ~39-55; used by `EngineeringTab.tsx` and `$quoteNumber.tsx`), and an unapproved capacity fails `CAPACITY_SIGNED_OFF`. So, for an actor holding `capacity.signoff`, treat a capacity status that is *not yet signed* (null, not `blocked`) as satisfiable: in `forwardReadiness`, in the server's `availableActions[].missing`, and in the decision endpoint, which records the sign-off first and then evaluates the rules. Ticking it records the sign-off by the lead and the decision in the same transaction (both audited), then the G2 rules run. When capacity is blocked, the dialog shows the block note and Advance is unavailable. The lead no longer needs the separate "Approve capacity" button; the Capacity sign-off card shows the status to everyone and the buttons only to the scheduler (and to the lead when no scheduler is assigned).
