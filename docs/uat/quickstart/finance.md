# QuoteFlow quick-start: Finance (payback model)

**What QuoteFlow is for you.** Quotes that need a payback model before the price is approved: capital is required, $0 CER included. Pricing uploads the costing model (costs and the initial price assumptions); you build the payback from it on your template and upload the payback model. You are an input, not an approver: the pricing lead approves the price (G3, **Price approved**) once your payback model is on file.

**Your home screen.** **Payback requests**, with what to do. Your menu: **Home**, **Pipeline**. Your role reads quotes, uploads the payback model and may comment on a quote; it changes nothing else.

## What you'll do

1. **Get told.** When Pricing uploads a costing model on a quote that needs a payback model, you get a bell notice and an email: "… is ready for its payback model". It opens the quote's **Quoting** tab.
2. **Take the assumptions.** **Costing, payback and price approval** → **1. Costing**: download the current costing model (the latest version is flagged **current**).
3. **Upload the payback model.** **2. Payback** says why the payback model is needed. Click **Upload the payback model**. The pricing lead is told.
4. **Check the summary.** After the upload, QuoteFlow shows a short summary of the headline figures it read from your workbook. It is read-only: you cannot edit it in QuoteFlow. An Excel error, a negative or zero payback, or a blank headline figure shows as a warning. A warning never blocks anything.
5. **Costing changed?** If Pricing uploads a new costing model version, you are told again, and step 2 says when your payback model is older than the costing model. **Upload a new version**: the earlier one stays under **Earlier versions**.
6. **After the price is approved** the steps are locked. A send-back to Quoting reopens them.

## What changed

- Assumptions come from QuoteFlow, not from email: the costing model is on the quote.
- The payback is a step inside Quoting, after costing and before the price is approved (D-001). No payback model is needed when capital is not required; the step says **Not required** and why.
