# QuoteFlow quick-start: Finance (ROI)

**What QuoteFlow is for you.** Quotes that need an ROI before the price is approved: capital is required, $0 CER included. Pricing uploads the costing model (costs and the initial price assumptions); you build the ROI from it on your template and upload the ROI model. You are an input, not an approver: the pricing lead approves the price (G3, **Price approved**) once your ROI is on file.

**Your home screen.** **ROI requests**, with what to do. Your menu: **Home**, **Pipeline**. Your role reads quotes, uploads the ROI model and may comment on a quote; it changes nothing else.

## What you'll do

1. **Get told.** When Pricing uploads a costing model on a quote that needs an ROI, you get a bell notice and an email: "… is ready for its ROI". It opens the quote's **Quoting** tab.
2. **Take the assumptions.** **Costing, ROI and price approval** → **1. Costing**: download the current costing model (the latest version is flagged **current**).
3. **Upload the ROI.** **2. ROI** says why the ROI is needed. Click **Upload the ROI model**. The pricing lead is told.
4. **Costing changed?** If Pricing uploads a new costing model version, you are told again, and step 2 says when your ROI is older than the costing model. **Upload a new version**: the earlier one stays under **Earlier versions**.
5. **After the price is approved** the steps are locked. A send-back to Quoting reopens them.

## What changed

- Assumptions come from QuoteFlow, not from email: the costing model is on the quote.
- The ROI is a step inside Quoting, after costing and before the price is approved (D-001). No ROI is needed when capital is not required; the step says **Not required** and why.
