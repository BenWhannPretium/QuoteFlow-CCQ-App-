# QuoteFlow quick-start: Engineer

**What QuoteFlow is for you.** The engineering reviews assigned to you, with the intake, the drawing and the KAM's notes in one place. You record the solution. The capacity evidence is yours too unless a production scheduler is assigned to the quote: each card says whose part it is (**Your part**, **Production scheduler's part**). Your lead decides G2.

**Your home screen: My assignments.** Your reviews are listed first, then the whole queue (read-only). You get a notification and an email when work is assigned to you. Your menu: **Home**, **My assignments**, **Pipeline**.

## What you'll do

1. **Accept the review.** Open the quote → **Engineering** tab → **Accept (start review)**.
2. **Record the solution.** Pick the **Plant / site** first, then **Process & machine type**: the list shows only what that plant runs, and the process fills in by itself. Fill **Cavitation**, **Cycle time (s)**, **Budgetary capital estimate (USD)** and the notes. Click **Save solution**. The product facts from intake are shown at the top: you don't retype them. Upload the **equipment BOM** (the one-page CSR BOM) in its own card; a new upload keeps the earlier versions. Enter **0** in the capital estimate when no capital is needed. When the estimate is above $0, the equipment BOM is needed before the quote can advance. If the part is made in two steps, tick **Two-step process** and fill the plants and preform number; add the **pack-out** if you have it: one of units per bag, tray or case (bag and case are not used together), and units per pallet. Pricing reads both. Enter the **Tooling funding model** and **MOQ** on the quote's **Overview** → **Commercial terms**: they are Engineering's to fill.
3. **Add capacity evidence.** **Add capacity row**: site, line, utilization before, allocated annual volume, run days, assumed effectiveness, cycle time, analysis date, analyst. Click **Save capacity evidence**. Utilization after is calculated for you: green up to the target, amber above it, red above 100%. A number out of range is flagged as you type and cannot be saved. **Download CSV** exports the rows; **Attach analysis** adds your own file.
4. **Attach files.** **Attachments** tab: drag CAD files, PDFs or photos onto the drop area.
5. **Submit to your lead.** When your part is ready, click **Submit to engineering lead**. It is locked for you until they decide or send it back. **If capacity is blocked**, the work comes back to you at once with the blocker's note at the top of the tab: rework the solution and submit again.

## What changed from the ERF

- Work comes to you by assignment, with an alert, not by email.
- Capacity evidence is recorded with the quote, not in a separate file.
- You can read back every figure you enter, and the pricing on the quote. Only Pricing changes pricing.
