# QuoteFlow quick-start: Leadership

> Checked against the build on 2026-11-17 (pre-release audit): the overview stat reads **Median: intake to Final Review decision**.

**What QuoteFlow is for you.** A view of every commercial capital quote: where it is, how long it takes, what we win and lose, and how the rules are followed. You see everything, including pricing and capital figures. You do not change them. You decide two things: payment-terms exceptions of Net 60 and above, and the finance review of amortized tooling (both provisional, standing in for the CFO and Finance until those approvers are set).

**Your Command Center: Leadership overview.** It shows **Open quotes**, **Median: intake to Final Review decision**, **First-pass intake completeness**, **Win rate and outcome capture** and **Pipeline by stage**. At the top are two views: **My work** and **Analytics**. Your menu: **Command Center**, **Engineering queue**, **Pricing queue**, **Pipeline**, **Approvals**, **Admin**.

## What you'll do

1. **Read the analytics.** **Command Center** → **Analytics** → **Leadership report**: Pipeline, Outcomes, Cycle time, Flow, Reasons, Quality and governance, Priority, Adoption. Click the ⓘ on any chart for its definition and time window. Click **Table** to see the numbers.
2. **Review overrides.** **Admin** → **Override log**: every override, the original value, the reason and who decided.
3. **Check the audit trail.** **Admin** → **Audit log**: every view, change, decision, export and sign-in.
4. **Export.** **Pipeline** → **Export CSV** (every export is audited).
5. **Decide a payment-terms exception.** When a KAM or Pricing enters Net 60, Net 90, Net 120 or Other, you are told. Open the notice: it goes to the quote's **Commercial terms** tab. Read the **supporting document** (Payment-terms approval), write a **Rationale** and **Approve the exception** or **Reject**. You cannot approve until the document is attached; the person who entered the terms attaches it.
6. **Give the finance review.** A quote whose tooling is **Customer funded (amortized)** needs your **Sign off** in Final Review (**Finance review (Leadership)**) before the offer can be released.

## What changed from the ERF

- Outcomes are reason-coded, so win and loss reasons are reportable.
- The win rate is shown next to the outcome capture rate.
- Overrides are logged with a justification and can be reverted.
- These numbers are operational, not D&A-certified.
