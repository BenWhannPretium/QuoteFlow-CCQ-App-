# QuoteFlow quick-start: Production scheduler

**What QuoteFlow is for you.** The capacity part of a new business quote. The engineering lead assigns you to a quote next to the engineer. The engineer picks the plant and the machine; you say whether the line has room, and approve or block capacity. This role is new and provisional (FB-16): tell Bailey what does not fit how you work.

**Your Command Center: Capacity requests.** The quotes whose capacity you own, each with where capacity stands, then the whole engineering queue (read-only). Your menu: **Command Center**, **Capacity requests**, **Pipeline**. You can read every quote, including its figures. You change only the capacity part of the quotes assigned to you.

## What you'll do

1. **Get told.** New quotes show a **New** chip in **Capacity requests** until you save a capacity row (**New to me** lists them). You get a bell notice and an email when the lead assigns you ("capacity is assigned to you"), and again when the engineer has saved the plant and the machine type ("Capacity can start", with the plant and the machine type). You can start before that: nothing locks you out.
2. **Open your part.** **Capacity requests** → the quote. It opens on the **Capacity** tab, your own tab (a dot on the tab means work is waiting for you). It opens with **Capacity recommendation**: whether capacity is signed off or blocked, by whom and when, the note, and each capacity row in one line. Below it: who works capacity, the engineer's solution the rows are calculated with, **Capacity evidence** and **Capacity sign-off** (marked **Your part**), and your Submit. The **Solution** is on the **Engineering** tab, the **Engineer's part**: you can read it, not change it.
3. **Add capacity evidence.** **Add capacity row**: site (the plants that run the chosen machine type), line, utilization before, allocated annual volume, run days, assumed effectiveness, cycle time, analysis date. Click **Save capacity evidence**: **Progress saved** appears beside the button. Utilization after is calculated for you: green up to the target, amber above it, red above 100%. A number out of range (for example 750% effectiveness) is flagged as you type and cannot be saved.
4. **Approve capacity.** In **Capacity sign-off** click **Approve capacity**. **Capacity recommendation** at the top of the tab now says **Capacity is signed off**. The engineering lead can then advance the quote.
5. **Or block it.** Type why in **Sign-off note** and click **Block capacity**. The work goes straight back to the engineer with your note; the engineering lead and the KAM are told. When the engineer has reworked the solution, come back and approve.
6. **Submit capacity to the lead.** When your rows are done, click **Submit capacity to engineering lead** at the end of the capacity part (it lists what is missing until then). The lead is told once the engineer has submitted too; the engineer gets a bell notice. Your rows are read-only until the lead sends them back.
7. **If the lead sends it back to you.** You get a notice with the lead's note. Fix your rows, save and submit capacity again. The engineer's part stays with the lead.

## What changed

- Capacity has its own **Capacity** tab, apart from Engineering (Nov 19 feedback): the capacity recommendation and the engineering recommendation are shown separately, on the quote, the Overview, the Quoting tab and Final Review. The decision on the quote (G2) is still one decision, made by the engineering lead on the Engineering tab.
- Capacity is recorded on the quote, with the solution it belongs to, not in a separate file or an email.
- A block no longer leaves a quote waiting: it returns to the engineer at once, with the reason.
- Changing the solution does not clear your approval. If the plant, the machine or the cycle time changes after you approved, check capacity again.
