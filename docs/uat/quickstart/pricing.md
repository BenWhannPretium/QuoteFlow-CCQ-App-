# QuoteFlow quick-start: Pricing and the pricing lead

**What QuoteFlow is for you.** The quotes Engineering has cleared, ready to price. You upload the costing model, record the price approval and capital readiness, and the pricing lead approves the price (gate G3, **Price approved**) to send the quote to Final Review. Costing stays in Excel and the pricing app; QuoteFlow keeps the files. Capital readiness is its own field, not part of the gate.

**Your home screen.** Analysts see **Pricing desk**. The pricing lead sees **Pricing triage**. Both show **Quoting queue**, **Capital readiness pending** and **Median time in Quoting**. Your menu: **Home**, **Pricing queue**, **Pipeline**, **Analytics**.

## What you'll do

1. **Pricing lead: assign pricers.** **Pricing queue** → row menu **…** → **Assign pricer…**. Use **Reassign work…** to balance the load.
2. **Step 1, costing: upload the costing model.** On the **Quoting** tab, **Costing, ROI and price approval** → **1. Costing** → **Upload the costing model** (one workbook: costs and the initial price assumptions). A changed model? **Upload a new version**: the earlier one stays under **Earlier versions**. When capital is required ($0 CER included), Finance is told to build the ROI.
3. **Record the price approval.** Check the **Engineering summary** and **Pricing history**. Tick **Costing completed (Cost-to-Serve)** (it needs the costing model on file), set **Approved by** and **Approved at**, then **Save price approval**.
4. **Record capital readiness.** Pick a **Status** (a pending status needs a justification). Click **Save capital readiness**.
5. **Check the terms.** On **Overview** → **Commercial terms**, fill payment terms and tooling commitment, tick **Terms checked** and **Save terms**. Final Review shows an open warning if it is not done. The funding list includes **$0 CER (customer-reimbursed capital)** and **Shared**.
6. **Submit to the pricing lead.** Pricers click **Submit to pricing lead**. It is locked for you until the lead decides or sends it back.
7. **Step 2, ROI (Finance).** When the quote needs one, **2. ROI** says why; Finance uploads the ROI model and the pricing lead is told. Otherwise it says **Not required**.
8. **Step 3, pricing lead: approve the price (G3).** **Ready for my review** lists what was submitted. Optionally **Upload an updated price table** if the price changed (otherwise the price in the models stands). Choose **Price approved: advance to Final Review…** (it needs the costing model and, when required, the ROI model), **Return to Engineering…**, **Return to KAM…** or **Hold or stop…** (Hold, Rework (re-cost), Redirect, Stop). Every send-back, hold and stop needs a note. Once you advance, the pricing section is locked until a send-back reopens it.

## What changed from the ERF

- The preliminary capital figure is now the **Budgetary capital estimate**. There is no CER reference box.
- No price figures are typed into QuoteFlow. You record who approved, when, the basis and the range.
- BPR is retired. The legacy ERF # is optional.
- Costing, then ROI, then price approval (D-001): the costing model and, when capital is required, Finance's ROI model are on file before the price is approved. Each model keeps its versions; the latest is flagged.
- Quotes already in Quoting with costing attested before this change show **No model on file** and keep working.
- You never decide Final Review on a quote you priced.
