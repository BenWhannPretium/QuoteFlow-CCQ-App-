# QuoteFlow quick-start: Pricing and the pricing lead

> Checked against the build on 2026-11-17 (pre-release audit): the menu item reads **Assign pricer**, **Costing completed** is ticked for you when the costing model is uploaded, and submit to lead appears once nothing is missing. Updated 2026-11-17 for the approved UX changes (docs/audit/03-ux-proposals.md). Updated 2026-11-18 for FB-21: capital readiness is filled in and pending never blocks, a payback model only on request, **Submit to pricing lead** also at the top, Engineering first on the Quoting tab.

**What QuoteFlow is for you.** The quotes Engineering has cleared, ready to price. You upload the costing model, record the price approval and capital readiness, and the pricing lead approves the price (gate G3, **Price approved**) to send the quote to Final Review. Costing stays in Excel and the pricing app; QuoteFlow keeps the files. Capital readiness is its own field, not part of the gate.

**Your Command Center.** Analysts see **Pricing desk**. The pricing lead sees **Pricing triage**. Both show **Quoting queue**, **Capital readiness pending** and **Median time in Quoting**. At the top are two views: **My work** and **Analytics**. Your menu: **Command Center**, **Pricing queue**, **Pipeline**.

## What you'll do

1. **Pricing lead: assign pricers.** **Pricing queue** → row menu **…** → **Assign pricer**. Use **Reassign work…** to balance the load. You may assign a quote to yourself, price it and approve it.
2. **Read what Engineering and the KAM handed over.** A quote in Quoting opens on the **Quoting** tab for you, and the line under the stage tracker says whose move it is. The tab starts with Engineering's hand-over: **Engineering summary** (decision, process, machine, plant, cavitation, cycle time, budgetary capital, capacity evidence; a row shows only when it has a value), then **From engineering** (equipment BOM, two-step, pack-out). Then **From intake** shows the payment terms the customer asked for (a non-standard request is flagged **Non-standard: Pricing will review**: it is for awareness and negotiation and does not change the costing), whether the customer funds any tooling, returnable or single-trip dunnage, delivered or FOB with the freight type and any special arrangement (drop trailers and T-bars are costly), the bid / program reference, whether the customer commits to the tooling and whether it wants stock held (and for how long: an extra cost). A **Concept** badge means the customer sent a render, not a drawing: read the KAM's **Concept assumptions**. A blank means the KAM did not say: ask.
3. **Step 1, costing: upload the costing model.** On the **Quoting** tab, **Costing, payback and price approval** → **1. Costing** → **Upload the costing model** (one workbook: costs and the initial price assumptions; an Excel workbook (.xlsx), up to 100 MB). A changed model? **Upload a new version**: the earlier one stays under **Earlier versions**. Uploading it does not tell Finance.
4. **Record the price approval.** Check the **Engineering summary** and **Pricing history**. **Costing completed (Cost-to-Serve)** is ticked for you when the costing model is uploaded (untick it if the costing is not final). Set **Approved by** and **Approved at**, then **Save price approval**. **Progress saved** appears beside the button, as it does beside every Save.
5. **Check capital readiness.** The **Status** is filled in when the quote reaches Quoting: **Capital pending (budgetary)** when Engineering's budgetary capital is above $0, **Capital not required** at $0. Change it if you need to and click **Save capital readiness**. Pending is normal before award (no CER is raised until the customer awards) and never holds up the price; the **Note on pending capital** is optional. **Capital not approved** means the quote should stop.
6. **Check the terms.** On the **Commercial terms** tab, check the payment terms (the customer's request, else Net 30), pick the tooling payment, answer the tooling commitment, tick **Terms checked** and **Save terms**. A payment term other than Net 15 or Net 30 goes to the pricing lead as an exception, to approve or reject with a rationale. You can pre-fill the **Capital terms**: the approver confirms them. Funding and MOQ are Engineering's. Final Review shows an open warning if the terms check is not done.
7. **Submit to the pricing lead.** Pricers click **Submit to pricing lead** at the top of the **Quoting** tab ("Your part is done?") or at the bottom: it is the same action. Until nothing is missing, the top line says what is and links to the list at the bottom. Save first: while a card has unsaved changes the button is off and says "Save your changes first." It is locked for you until the lead decides or sends it back. The lead is told in QuoteFlow and by email.
8. **Step 2, Payback: only when you ask.** **2. Payback** reads **Not requested** until someone asks. If you need Finance's payback model, the assigned pricer or the pricing lead clicks **Ask Finance for a payback model** (once the costing model is uploaded), with an optional note. Finance is told, and the price approval waits for Finance's upload. When it is in, whoever asked and the pricing leads are told. No longer needed? **Withdraw request** with a short reason: Finance is told and the price stops waiting. A hint ("Finance’s payback model may be useful here: …") is only a suggestion.
9. **Step 3, pricing lead: approve the price (G3).** **Ready for my review** lists what was submitted. Optionally **Upload an updated price table** if the price changed (otherwise the price in the models stands). Click **Decide price approval** (it appears once the costing model and, if someone asked Finance for one, the payback model are on file; until then the button reads **Send back, hold or stop**) and choose **Advance – price approved**, **Send back to…** (Pricing (re-cost), Engineering, the KAM, or Pricing (another capital model)), **Hold** or **Stop**. Every send-back needs a reason and a note; a stop needs a reason and a note; a hold needs a reason, a review date and a note. Capital pending does not stop you; **Capital not approved** does: stop the quote, or change capital readiness. Once you advance, the pricing section is locked until a send-back reopens it.

## What changed from the ERF

- The preliminary capital figure is now the **Budgetary capital estimate**. There is no CER reference box.
- No price figures are typed into QuoteFlow. You record who approved, when, the basis and the range.
- BPR is retired. The legacy ERF # is optional.
- Costing, then payback, then price approval (D-001): the costing model and, when someone asked Finance for one, Finance's payback model are on file before the price is approved. Each model keeps its versions; the latest is flagged.
- Quotes already in Quoting with costing attested before this change show **No model on file** and keep working.
- You never decide Final Review on a quote you priced.
