- **Capital readiness is filled in for you** when a quote reaches Quoting: **Capital pending (budgetary)** when Engineering's budgetary capital is above $0 (or blank), **Capital not required** when every item's figure is $0. You can change it.
- **Pending is normal before award** (no CER until the customer awards). It never stops the price approval, and the old "Pending-capital justification" is now **Note on pending capital (optional)**.
- **Capital not approved** means the quote should stop: the pricing lead cannot advance it, only stop it or change capital readiness.

- **Engineering's hand-over now comes first** on the Quoting tab.
- Each model upload says "Excel workbook (.xlsx), up to 100 MB".
